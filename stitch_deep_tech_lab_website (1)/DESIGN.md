# Xylvanthrex Labs — Frontier Deep Tech Research Monograph

## 1. Design Philosophy: Frontier Editorial & Silent Authority
The lab is an advanced fundamental research institution operating at the frontier of physics, compute, autonomous intelligence, and synthetic biology. The website must embody "the biggest, deepest tech lab" through pure architectural conviction:
- Structure emerges solely from space, surface tone gradation, and precise radius. Absolutely zero decorative borders or line dividers.
- Editorial rigor meets speculative deep science: typographic authority (Newsreader serif for conceptual depth, IBM Plex Sans for legibility, IBM Plex Mono for technical taxonomy).
- Tonal hierarchy: warm paper base (`#f3efe5`) and stratified mineral surfaces (`#fbf8f1`, `#f0ebe0`), inverted deep obsidian slate (`#17170f`) for fundamental compute/monolith nodes.
- One singular signal pigment (`#a6391a` terracotta cinnabar) designating active research breakthroughs, state verifications, and quantum telemetry.

## 2. Color System
- `--paper`: #f3efe5 (warm monograph ground)
- `--paper-deep`: #ebe5d6 (secondary ground)
- `--surface`: #fbf8f1 (primary raised research tile)
- `--surface-2`: #f0ebe0 (nested instrumentation layer)
- `--surface-3`: #e4ddcc (tertiary neutral, hover states)
- `--ink`: #17170f (deep cosmic obsidian, monolith panels)
- `--ink-2`: #3d3c32 (prose text)
- `--muted`: #6d6a5d (epistemic labels, metadata)
- `--signal`: #a6391a (active state indicator, singular accent)
- `--signal-soft`: #f5ded3 (status pill active tint)
- `--signal-deep`: #8b2f14 (status pill text)
- `--on-ink`: #f7f3e9 (monograph light on dark)
- `--on-ink-soft`: rgba(247, 243, 233, 0.72)

## 3. Typography
- Display / Conceptual: Newsreader (serif, tracking -0.03em, tight line height)
- Body / Technical Prose: IBM Plex Sans
- Metadata / Telemetry / Labels: IBM Plex Mono (uppercase, 0.14em tracking, small caps)

## 4. Geometry & Elevation
- Radii: `--r-xl`: 32px, `--r-lg`: 22px, `--r-md`: 14px, `--r-pill`: 999px.
- Shadows: Soft diffuse warm atmospheric lift (`0 20px 48px rgba(23,23,15,0.06)`), never harsh outlines.
- No borders. Transitions between structural modules happen through negative space and tonal depth steps.
